Resveratrol timing in a fruit fly model of diabetes and prenatal alcohol exposure

High school · Animal science · 2nd in category at LISEF and NYSSEF

Maternal diabetes and prenatal alcohol exposure both disrupt development through oxidative stress and metabolic dysfunction. I studied how the two interact in Drosophila melanogaster, a fly with conserved insulin signaling pathways, and whether resveratrol protects differently when it’s given maternally, postnatally, or both.

The study used 500 flies across 10 groups. A 20% sucrose diet modeled diabetes, and larval ethanol exposure modeled prenatal alcohol. I measured eclosion timing, climbing, respiration, and food-seeking.

A single camera can’t follow a fly that circles behind the vial or overlaps another fly. After reaching out to J_BlRD, I implemented the multi-view epipolar geometry from his open-source motion capture system to triangulate each fly’s 3D position from several low-cost cameras. That let me track every climb in the negative geotaxis assay without losing sight of a fly.

Results

  • Ethanol delayed eclosion by 33%.
  • Together, ethanol and diabetes lowered respiration by 33% and food-seeking by 23%.
  • Maternal resveratrol restored climbing in diabetic, ethanol-exposed flies to control levels; postnatal treatment left a significant deficit (p = .002).
  • Postnatal resveratrol sped up development the most, by 2.45 days.
  • Combined maternal and postnatal treatment gave the largest metabolic recovery (+17.4%), though respiration stayed below baseline.

p < .001 unless noted.

Front-desk system for a medical clinic

High school · Sole developer

I worked at the clinic as a medical assistant and secretary. I built a digital system to replace its paper one, covering appointment reminders, callback tracking, prescription workflows, and filing. The staff adopted it.

After running the front desk alone for about a month, I concluded that the real bottleneck was staff overload rather than tooling. When the clinic got busier, I implemented the streamlined version, and it is still in use.
